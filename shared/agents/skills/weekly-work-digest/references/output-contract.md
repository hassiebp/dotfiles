# Weekly digest output contract

Use this structure for scheduled and team-ready output.

## Two-minute story

Write a coherent narrative with:

1. the week's central theme;
2. two or three concrete investments;
3. their impact on users, reliability, velocity, or architecture;
4. one closing sentence that connects the work.

Target 200 to 280 words. Avoid reading out PR numbers.

## Three money pieces

For each item include:

- **Outcome** — what changed or was learned;
- **Impact** — why the team should care;
- **State** — shipped, in review, investigated, or blocked.

## Topic appendix

Group items under a small number of stable themes. For every item, prefer:

`Result — state — link`

Mention validation or evidence only when it changes confidence in the result.

## Attention next week

Include only items requiring action. Name the next action and owner when known. Omit the section when there is nothing material.
